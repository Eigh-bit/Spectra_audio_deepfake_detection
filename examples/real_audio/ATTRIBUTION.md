# Audio attribution

The three WAV files in `input/` are unchanged recordings from the
[Free Spoken Digit Dataset](https://github.com/Jakobovski/free-spoken-digit-dataset),
by Zohar Jackson and FSDD contributors. The filenames identify speakers Jackson,
Nicolas and Theo. Pinned revision and individual source URLs are in `inputs.json`.

No edits were made to the distributed audio bytes. The project's MIT license
does not replace the recordings' CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. Waveform figures derived
from these recordings are also provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 with this attribution.

The samples are human spoken digits, not a dedicated anti-spoofing benchmark.
No independent forensic ground truth or model training-set overlap check is claimed.
